Kombino Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Berk Bacalan (“Kombino”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data when you use the Kombino mobile application and related services.

1. Controller and Contact

The data controller is Berk Bacalan. For privacy requests, support, deletion requests, or questions, contact us at: berkbacalan@gmail.com.

2. Information We Process

  • Account information supplied through Sign in with Apple, including your Firebase user identifier and, where Apple shares it, relay email address or display name.
  • Photos you choose to upload, including outfit, face, wardrobe, and style-profile photos.
  • Style information you provide, such as height, weight, preferences, occasions, written briefs, wardrobe items, and feedback.
  • AI-generated analysis results, recommendations, scores, saved looks, and history.
  • Purchase and credit information, including product identifiers, transaction references, and credit balance. Payment card details are handled by Apple and are not received or stored by Kombino.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as app version, device language, diagnostic data, authentication data, App Check data, and service logs.

3. Facial and Image Data

Kombino may process photos containing your face, body, clothing, or other personal characteristics solely to provide style, beauty, makeup, sunglasses, accessory, and outfit recommendations requested by you. Kombino does not use uploaded photos to identify you, perform identity verification, make legal, medical, employment, insurance, credit, or similarly significant decisions, or create a facial-recognition database.

Uploaded analysis images are stored in private server storage and associated with your account so that your analysis history can be restored across devices. Access is restricted using account-level controls and protected image links. Do not upload photos of other people unless you have their permission.

4. Why We Use Information

  • To create, secure, restore, and administer your account.
  • To process your uploaded content and generate requested AI style analyses.
  • To maintain your analysis history, wardrobe, profile, and purchased credits.
  • To verify purchases, prevent fraud, secure the app, and enforce usage limits.
  • To respond to support requests and feedback.
  • To improve reliability, diagnose errors, and comply with legal obligations.

5. Legal Bases

Where applicable, we process personal data to perform our contract with you, with your consent, for our legitimate interests in operating and protecting Kombino, and where required to comply with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis; this does not affect prior lawful processing.

6. AI Providers and Service Providers

We use vetted service providers to operate Kombino, including authentication, database, hosting, crash/security infrastructure, purchase-management, and AI processing providers. These providers process data only as necessary to provide their services to us and under their applicable agreements and policies.

We do not intentionally use your uploaded photos or personal style data to train Kombino-owned AI models. Third-party processing may be subject to the applicable provider’s terms and privacy documentation.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information only to service providers, where required by law, to protect rights and safety, in connection with a merger or asset transfer, or with your express direction or consent.

8. International Transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Retention and Deletion

We retain account information, uploaded analysis images, results, and credit records while your account remains active or as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your Kombino data by contacting us. Certain transaction, security, and legal records may be retained where required or permitted by law.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including authenticated access controls and private storage. No system, transmission, or storage method is completely secure; you use the service at your own risk to the extent permitted by law.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, withdraw consent, receive a portable copy of your data, or lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Submit requests through the contact information above. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

12. Children

Kombino is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required in their jurisdiction. Do not use Kombino if you do not meet the applicable minimum age.

13. Third-Party Links

Kombino may link to third-party services. We are not responsible for their content, security, or privacy practices.

14. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted in the app or on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the updated policy to the extent permitted by law.

15. Contact

Privacy questions: berkbacalan@gmail.com
