Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Backdoor Outdoor ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, offline navigation experience.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Backdoor Outdoor is designed with privacy as its core principle. The App operates as a standalone utility, and your personal data remains under your absolute control:

  • Location Data: The App accesses your device’s GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude, and accuracy) to plot your route, display your live coordinates, and enable backtrack navigation. All location data is processed entirely on-device and stored locally in your device's secure storage. We do not transmit, collect, upload, or sell your location data to external servers or third parties.
  • Motion and Sensor Data: The App accesses your device’s compass, accelerometer, and motion sensors to orient the map and radar screen to your physical heading and compute step lengths in Pedometer mode. This data is processed in real-time, strictly on-device, and is never stored permanently or uploaded.
  • Route Logs and Waypoints: Any saved hikes, backtrack points, and points of interest (POIs) you define are stored solely in your local device database (Isar). They remain on your device unless you choose to delete them or uninstall the App.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To provide subscription verification, analytics, and crash monitoring, we integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs). These services collect anonymized, non-personally identifiable telemetry data:

  • RevenueCat: Used to validate and manage your premium subscriptions. Transaction details, anonymous user identifiers, and purchasing receipts are processed securely through RevenueCat in accordance with their privacy guidelines.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Used to monitor application performance, track feature usage events, and diagnose software crashes. No raw location, sensor, or personal identity data is shared with Firebase.

3. Data Security and Offline Standalone Nature

Because your coordinates and hike history do not leave your physical device, your data is inherently protected from database breaches, cloud leaks, or server-side exploits. You are responsible for maintaining the physical security of your mobile device to prevent unauthorized local access to your data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is transmitted off-device, the App is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): Since we do not transmit, store, or process your personal location coordinates on our servers, we do not maintain "personal data" databases. You hold the absolute right to view, modify, and delete all local hike logs directly within the App's settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
