Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Calorist is an AI-powered calorie tracking app operated by Berk Bacalan. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

Calorist may process meal photos selected by the user, AI-generated meal analysis results, calorie and macronutrient entries, app settings such as language and appearance, subscription status, device-level analytics events, and optional in-app feedback.

Meal Photos and AI Analysis

When a user selects a meal photo for analysis, the photo is sent to Google AI services to estimate calories, macronutrients, visible food items and related notes. Meal photos may also be stored locally on the user's device so the app can show meal history. Calorist does not use meal photos for identity verification.

Analytics and Usage Tracking

Calorist uses Firebase Analytics and Firestore to understand app usage, improve product quality, monitor analysis usage, and collect optional feedback. Analytics events may include actions such as opening the app, selecting a photo, starting or completing an analysis, opening the paywall, restoring purchases, and submitting feedback.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Calorist uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status, display paywalls, restore purchases and verify premium access. RevenueCat and Apple process subscription purchase information. Calorist does not receive or store full payment card details.

Third-Party Services

Calorist may share limited information with service providers needed to operate the app, including Google AI services, Firebase, RevenueCat and Apple App Store services. These providers process data according to their own privacy and security practices.

Data Retention

Meal history stored locally remains on the user's device unless the user deletes individual analyses, clears history, or removes the app. Analytics, usage and feedback records may be retained as needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

User Choices

Users can delete analyses from the app, clear local history, choose not to submit optional feedback, manage subscriptions through Apple, and uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from the device.

Health Disclaimer

Calorist provides estimated nutrition information for general wellness and tracking purposes only. It is not medical advice and should not be used to diagnose, treat or manage a medical condition.

Children

Calorist is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
